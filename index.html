How Verification Works

Four steps to a
verified attempt

The reason a Rs. 299 entry can carry real prize money: every lift is watched by someone with skin in the game before it ever reaches a judge.

01 / REGISTER

Pick your category

Sign up under your bodyweight class and pick the lift — squat, bench, deadlift or a physique round. Pay the entry fee for that tier.

02 / WEIGH-IN

Scale, on camera

Visit your nearest partner gym. Your bodyweight and the loaded barbell are weighed on a calibrated scale, on camera, before you lift.

03 / LIFT, WITNESSED

Trainer sign-off

The gym's trainer stands in frame, confirms the lift live, and co-signs the submission with their partner-gym ID. No recorded, edited or borrowed footage.

04 / RESULT

Ranked & rewarded

Your verified lift is ranked inside your gym, then your city. Certificates auto-generate; cash & gear go out to category toppers.

Why Entries Can't Be Faked

Built to stop three kinds of cheating

The whole model exists because prize money only means something if the lift is real.

  • 01

    Under-declared bodyweight

    Bodyweight is checked live on a calibrated scale at the partner gym, on camera, at weigh-in — not self-reported.

  • 02

    Fake or mismatched plates

    Only certified plate sets are accepted; each plate is shown to camera before the lift, and the loaded bar is scale-weighed on video.

  • 03

    Pre-recorded or edited footage

    Submissions are continuous, timestamped, live-to-server clips — no gaps, no cuts, no uploads from the gallery.

  • 04

    The human backstop

    A partner-gym trainer physically witnesses and co-signs every attempt before it's queued for judge review.
