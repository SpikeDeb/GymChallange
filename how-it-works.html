Fair Play, By Design

How we make sure the weight on the bar is the weight in the record

The submission checklist

STEP 01

Weigh-in on camera

Bodyweight and the fully-loaded barbell are placed on a calibrated scale and weighed on live video, before the attempt begins.

STEP 02

Plates shown individually

Every plate is held up to camera with its marking visible. Only certified plate sets are accepted — mismatched or unlabeled plates void the attempt.

STEP 03

Full-body framing

The entire lift, start to lockout, must stay inside frame. Partial reps or camera angles that hide the movement are rejected.

STEP 04

Continuous, timestamped feed

Recording streams live to PRAMAAN's server with a running timestamp overlay. No pausing, no gallery uploads, no edited clips.

STEP 05

Trainer co-sign

A partner-gym trainer stays in frame and confirms the attempt with their Partner ID — a human witness backing every submission.

STEP 06

Judge review

A review panel checks lockout, form and plate-to-bar consistency using AI-assisted plate detection as a first filter, human sign-off as the final word.

What Gets Flagged

Three failure modes we actively guard against

  • 01

    Under-declared bodyweight

    Solved with a scale-verified, on-camera weigh-in at a partner gym — never a self-reported number.

  • 02

    Light or fake plates dressed as heavy ones

    Certified plates only, individually shown to camera, plus a scale-weighed loaded bar and AI plate-size cross-checks flag mismatches for manual review.

  • 03

    Recycled or edited footage

    Live-to-server streaming with a timestamp overlay makes pre-recorded or spliced clips fail verification automatically.

Top Prizes

High-value winners get a second look

Category toppers competing for the largest prize pools are invited to a physical re-verification round at a regional partner gym before payout — an extra layer for the cases where the financial stakes are highest.
