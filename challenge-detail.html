Submission Rules
  • 01

    Weigh-in first

    Bodyweight and fully-loaded bar weighed on a calibrated scale, on camera, at a partner gym before the attempt.

  • 02

    Certified plates only

    Each plate shown to camera individually before loading. Non-standard plates disqualify the attempt.

  • 03

    Continuous live footage

    No cuts, no gallery uploads. Recording streams straight to PRAMAAN from weigh-in through lockout.

  • 04

    Trainer sign-off

    Your partner gym's trainer must be in frame and co-sign the submission with their Partner ID.

  • 05

    Judge review

    A panel confirms lockout, form and no assistance before the lift is ranked. Results within 48 hours.
